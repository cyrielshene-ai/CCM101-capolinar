# Mission 3 Reflection

Completing Mission 3 helped me understand that choosing a cloud platform is not simply about selecting the most popular provider. Each platform has different strengths, services, and technologies that can match specific business requirements. Through the research and comparison activities, I gained a better understanding of AWS, Microsoft Azure, and Google Cloud Platform.

The cloud platform that impressed me the most was Google Cloud Platform (GCP). I found its capabilities in Artificial Intelligence, Machine Learning, data analytics, and Kubernetes especially interesting. Learning that Google developed Kubernetes also helped me understand why GCP is a strong platform for organizations that use containerized applications and modern cloud technologies.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities. All three provide computing, storage, networking, identity management, databases, security, and scalable cloud infrastructure. They also provide services that allow organizations to deploy applications without maintaining traditional physical data centers.

Business requirements strongly influence cloud platform selection. For example, a university that already uses Windows Server, Microsoft 365, and Active Directory may benefit more from Azure because of its Microsoft integration. An AI research company may prefer GCP because of its AI and Machine Learning capabilities, while a global e-commerce company may choose AWS because of its broad service portfolio and global infrastructure.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant taught me to analyze the client's actual needs before recommending a technology. I learned that factors such as scalability, existing systems, workload requirements, availability, and business goals should be considered when making cloud decisions.

My GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio also improved during this mission. I added research documents, comparison tables, client recommendations, a decision matrix, Linux investigation results, screenshots, and this reflection. This made my portfolio more organized and demonstrated my growing understanding of cloud computing and technical documentation.
